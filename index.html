Welcome to Kahawa West!

Greetings, residents of Kahawa West.This is a community research project focused on understanding and improving our beloved community.

This project aims to identify solvable problems and challenges within our community and find practical, achievable solutions over time. Through understanding the needs of the community, taking action, and measuring our progress, we hope to create positive and lasting improvements that benefit everyone.

This is a student based project that will use community research to identify important local needs, develop practical solutions, and measure the impact of those solutions over time. Through your input you can help identify major issues in our community and together we can make Kahawa West better for everyone!

Why Are We Doing This?

We want to understand the needs of people in Kahawa West and find practical ways to make our community better for you, your friends, family,pets,co-workers...Everyone!

So please take some time to fill this survey so as to get your view on the community.
